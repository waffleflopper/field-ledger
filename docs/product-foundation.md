# Product Foundation

## Product Name

Field Ledger

## Product Promise

Field Ledger helps an individual Army property holder manage hand receipts, property items, active 2062s, item locations, assignees, and recurring item requirements from a mobile-first web app.

## Product Type

- Personal subscription SaaS.
- Individual account ownership.
- Army-adjacent vocabulary and workflows.
- Not an organization/team system in MVP.

## Core Concepts

- **Hand Receipt**: a named bucket of property items, with optional formal metadata.
- **Item**: one physical accountable item.
- **Contact**: an account-level assignee record. Only display name is required.
- **Location**: an optional reusable account-level place record.
- **2062 Assignment**: a formal signed-out document workflow using a DA Form 2062 scan.
- **Requirement**: an item-level recurring obligation such as maintenance, inspection, calibration, or replacement.
- **Activity**: user-visible recent history.
- **Audit Log**: architecture/internal record of meaningful state changes.

## Anti-Goals

- No organization/team collaboration in MVP.
- No official Army system-of-record claims.
- No PHI, classified, or sensitive operational data.
- No monorepo at the start.
- No offline editing in MVP.
- No dedicated Reports module in MVP.
- No item photos in MVP.
- No tags/categories in MVP.
- No generic SaaS analytics-first dashboard.
- No placeholder mega-route MVP.
